## 4. HOW A WINNING TRADE ENDS (asked and answered 18:28)
It rides. Once the trail arms, the target is pushed 3R **beyond the best price** every cycle and only ever
moves further away, so it outruns price and is never hit. The trade ends when price retraces by the predicted
band behind its best point. Three things can still end a runner early:
1. **An opposing order-book wall caps the target** (once the book recheck has fired, every cycle after). This
   is the only one that is a judgement call rather than a safety net, and it is the first place to look if
   the operator wants winners to run further.
2. **`MAX_POSITION_CANDLES=120`** force-closes at two hours.
3. **Reversal risk ≥ 0.80** takes profit at the mark — only when the forecast turns.

## 6. STANDING FACTS — do not re-derive these
- **`net = gross edge − fees`.** Nothing changed today creates edge. Every fix removed a way of losing money
  that was not edge-related.
- **Nothing price-derived predicts direction**: 48–51.6% over 10,859 samples. Scalpel's gross +0.006%/trade is
  indistinguishable from random entry. 300 variants, none net positive. Mean reversion 0/243. The ML learner
  is a negative result — machinery is reusable, do not wire it in.
- **The wall signal is real and is the only measured edge.** 1,981 wall endings, drift-controlled: an ask wall
  ending is followed by price falling further than a bid wall ending, t = +7.6 on the largest sample,
  consistent across three settings and every horizon past one minute. Only the 200x/20-min cell clears the
  maker fee and it has n=109. Needs more data, not a rewrite. ⚠️ The absorbed/pulled classifier is
  miscalibrated: 51,250 "pulled" to 3 "faded" and **zero "absorbed"** — calibrate before trading it.
- **Candle data is sound.** A candle rebuilt live from the trade feed matched OKX's own candle for that minute
  exactly, o/h/l/c identical to the cent. Do not re-chase this.
- **TrapX is broken and is the next real bug.** It risks 0.17% to win 0.022%, a reward-to-risk of 0.13:1,
  which needs an 88% win rate at a zero fee. Two causes: the target picks the NEAREST opposing wall
  (`trade_module_trapx.py:62` and `:69` take `max()`/`min()` of the walls, which is the closest one), and
  `MAX_LIQUIDATION_THRESHOLD` is used as a raw dollar offset for the stop, which only looks sane because ETH
  trades near $2,400. Fix: give it a reward floor, pick the nearest wall that clears it, and replace the
  dollar constants with a fraction of price. Returning None when no wall qualifies is correct behaviour.
- The all-shorts bias is solved (`769c304`). Run 5 signals ran 38 LONG / 20 SHORT.
